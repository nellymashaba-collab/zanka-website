Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Last updated: 8 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd ("Zanka", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects personal information in connection with our website (zankagroup.co.za), our Owner, Tenant and Partner Portals, and our property management, tenant placement, investment advisory, maintenance management, lease administration and inspection services (together, the "Services").

This Policy applies to property owners, investors, tenants, guarantors, contractors, service providers, job applicants and other individuals whose personal information we process ("you").

This Policy should be read together with our POPIA Notice, which sets out the specific statutory notice required under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"), and our Terms & Conditions.

2. Who We Are

Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd is a South African property services and property management company, operating from Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa. For the purposes of POPIA, Zanka is the "responsible party" in respect of the personal information described in this Policy.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on your relationship with Zanka, we may collect:

  • Identification information: full name, ID number, date of birth;
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, physical or postal address;
  • Property and tenancy information: property address, ownership details, lease terms, rental amounts, occupancy status, inspection reports and photographs;
  • Financial information: rental payment history, invoices, statements, payment references, loan and property valuation details (for owners and investors), and affordability/credit-check results (for prospective tenants);
  • Account and portal information: login credentials, portal activity, uploaded documents;
  • Signature and verification information: e-signature data, OTP verification records, and the IP address and timestamp associated with signing a lease or other document;
  • Communications: messages sent via our contact form, email, WhatsApp or portal messaging, and records of correspondence; and
  • Technical information: device and browser information, and cookies (see section 11).

We do not intentionally collect special personal information (as defined in POPIA, including race, religion, health, trade union membership, political persuasion, sex life, criminal behaviour or biometric information) unless specifically required by law or with your explicit consent, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information without the consent of a competent person as required by POPIA.

4. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information:

  • Directly from you, when you complete a form on our website, register for a portal, sign a lease or other document, make a payment, or communicate with us;
  • From third parties, such as credit bureaus and tenant-screening agencies (for affordability and background checks), employers or references you provide, and co-parties to a lease (e.g. a guarantor, tenant or owner); and
  • Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies when you use our website.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide and administer our Services, including property management, tenant placement, lease administration, maintenance coordination and inspections;
  • Assess tenant applications, including affordability and credit checks;
  • Process payments and issue invoices, statements and receipts;
  • Prepare, send and manage lease and other legal documents, including electronic signature and verification;
  • Communicate with you about your property, tenancy, account or an enquiry, including via email and WhatsApp;
  • Comply with our legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations;
  • Maintain the security, integrity and proper functioning of our website and portals; and
  • Where you have consented, or you are an existing customer and have not opted out, send you relevant updates about our Services.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information on one or more of the following bases: performance of a contract with you (for example, a management agreement or lease); compliance with a legal obligation; your consent; and our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, provided this does not unjustifiably infringe your privacy.

7. Sharing Your Information

We may share personal information with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf ("operators" under POPIA), including our cloud hosting, database and application infrastructure provider (Supabase), our email service provider (Resend), our WhatsApp messaging provider (Twilio), and our payment gateway (PayFast);
  • Credit bureaus and tenant-screening agencies, for affordability and background checks;
  • Contractors, maintenance providers and other property professionals, to the extent necessary to deliver a service you have requested;
  • Co-parties to a lease (for example, an owner, tenant or guarantor), where necessary to administer that lease;
  • Professional advisors, such as attorneys and auditors, where necessary; and
  • Regulators, law enforcement or other parties, where required or permitted by law, or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell personal information.

8. Cross-Border Transfers

Some of our service providers, including our cloud hosting, email and messaging providers, may process personal information on servers located outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient is subject to a law, binding corporate rules, or an agreement that provides an adequate level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, in accordance with section 72 of POPIA.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, to comply with our legal, tax and accounting obligations (which for many financial and lease records is at least five years under South African law), and to resolve disputes. Where information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or de-identify it.

10. Security of Your Information

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, interference, modification or disclosure, including access controls, encryption in transit, and restricting access to personal information to individuals who need it to perform their role. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure; see also our Terms & Conditions.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and portals may use cookies and similar technologies necessary for login sessions, security and basic functionality. We do not currently use cookies for third-party advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may affect portal functionality.

12. Direct Marketing

Where we send you direct marketing communications, we will only do so with your consent, or where you are an existing customer and the communication relates to similar services, in accordance with section 69 of POPIA. You may opt out of direct marketing at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe mechanism in the communication.

13. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for use by adults (18 years or older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a competent person, as required by POPIA.

14. Your Rights

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

  • be notified that personal information about you is being collected, or has been accessed by an unauthorised person;
  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction, updating or deletion of your personal information;
  • object, on reasonable grounds, to the processing of your personal information;
  • object to your personal information being used for direct marketing;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 17. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

15. Complaints

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on our website with a revised effective date.
