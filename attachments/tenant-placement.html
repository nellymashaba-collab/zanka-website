Frequently Asked Questions

How does tenant placement work?

Zanka markets your property with professional photography and online listings, arranges viewings, screens applicants with credit checks, employment verification and affordability assessments, then prepares the lease and collects the deposit before handover to move-in inspection.

What does tenant placement cost?

Zanka's tenant placement fee is 75%–100% of one month's rental, charged once a tenant is successfully placed. It can be arranged as a standalone service or as part of onboarding a property into full management. See our Pricing page for details.

What is the difference between tenant placement and property management?

Tenant placement is a once-off service focused on finding and screening a tenant and preparing the lease. Property management is the ongoing service that follows: rent collection, tenant communication, maintenance and reporting for the life of the tenancy. Many landlords use tenant placement to fill a vacancy, then move into an ongoing property management package.

How are tenants screened?

Zanka conducts affordability assessments, credit checks, employment verification and reference checks before recommending a tenant, to reduce the risk of arrears or a problematic tenancy.

Does Zanka handle lease preparation for leasing in Johannesburg?

Yes. Lease preparation, deposit collection and the move-in inspection are included as part of the tenant placement process.
