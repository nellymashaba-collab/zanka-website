What Property Management With Zanka Covers

Property management is the day-to-day administration of a rental property on the owner's behalf. In Johannesburg's residential rental market, that typically means collecting rent reliably, keeping the property in good condition, managing the tenant relationship, and keeping the owner informed with regular reporting.

Zanka's property management service is built around four things: rent collection, tenant communication, maintenance coordination and lease administration, backed by monthly owner statements so you always know where you stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a property management company do?

A property management company handles the day-to-day running of a rental property on behalf of the owner. For Zanka, that means rent collection, tenant communication, maintenance coordination, lease administration, arrear management, deposit administration and property inspections, so the owner doesn't have to manage tenants or contractors directly.

How much does property management cost in Johannesburg?

Zanka's packages are priced as a percentage of monthly rent collected: Essential at 7%, Professional at 8% (our most popular tier), and Premium at 10%, with each tier adding more coverage such as inspections, lease administration and municipal payment handling. Once-off services like tenant placement, lease renewals and inspections are priced separately. See the full breakdown on our Pricing page.

What is included in property management?

Zanka's core service includes rent collection, monthly owner statements, tenant communication, maintenance coordination, lease administration, arrear management, deposit administration, contractor management and property inspections. Higher packages add quarterly inspections, municipal and levy payment handling, annual investment reviews and priority support.

Do property managers find tenants?

Yes. Zanka offers tenant placement as part of onboarding a new property, or as a standalone service, including marketing, screening, credit and affordability checks, and lease preparation. See our Tenant Placement page.

Who pays property management fees?

The property owner pays Zanka's management fee, typically deducted from the monthly rent collected before the balance is paid out to the owner.

What happens when a tenant stops paying rent?

Arrear management is included in Zanka's property management packages. We follow up with the tenant, manage communication and payment arrangements, and keep the owner informed throughout, in line with the applicable lease and South African law.

What areas of Johannesburg does Zanka service?

Zanka is based in Sandton and manages residential properties across Johannesburg, including Sandton, Kensington, Bedfordview, Germiston and the East Rand. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific property.
