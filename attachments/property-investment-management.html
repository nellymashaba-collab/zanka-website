Frequently Asked Questions

Can Zanka manage properties on behalf of investors?

Yes. Zanka combines investment advisory with day-to-day property management, so investors get both the financial analysis and the operational management of their rental properties under one relationship.

What is property investment management?

Property investment management combines investment advisory — rental yield analysis, cash flow forecasting, return on investment analysis and acquisition support — with the ongoing management of the property itself once it's acquired or already owned. It is sometimes called property asset management.

Is property investment management the same as property asset management?

At Zanka, yes — we treat these as one service. Investors need both the financial view (yield, cash flow, return on investment) and the operational view (a well-managed, well-maintained property), so we deliver them together rather than as separate offerings.

Does Zanka provide buy-to-let investment advice?

Zanka provides rental yield analysis, cash flow forecasting, return on investment analysis and property acquisition support for buy-to-let investors. This is general informational and analytical support, not financial, investment, legal or tax advice — investors should obtain independent professional advice before making significant decisions.

Can Zanka manage a multi-property portfolio?

Yes. Zanka's Premium package includes an annual investment review and is built for investors managing more than one property, consolidating reporting and management across a portfolio.
