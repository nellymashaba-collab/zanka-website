POPIA Notice

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This notice is given in terms of section 18 of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") and should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains in more detail how we handle personal information.

1. Responsible Party

Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd, of Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa, is the "responsible party" that determines the purpose and means of processing your personal information in connection with our website (zankagroup.co.za), our Owner, Tenant and Partner Portals, and our property management, tenant placement, investment advisory, maintenance management, lease administration and inspection services.

2. Information Officer

Zanka's Information Officer is responsible for ensuring compliance with POPIA and can be contacted at:

Email: admin@zankagroup.co.za
Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd, Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa

In terms of section 55 of POPIA, the Information Officer is required to be registered with the Information Regulator.

3. What We Collect, and Whether It's Required

When you provide personal information to Zanka — for example via our website, portals, or in connection with a lease or management agreement — please note:

  • The categories of personal information we typically collect (identification, contact, property and tenancy, financial, account, signature/verification, communications and technical information) are set out in section 3 of our Privacy Policy;
  • In most cases, information is collected directly from you; where we collect it from a third party — for example a credit bureau, employer, reference, or co-party to a lease — this is done for the purposes described in this notice and our Privacy Policy;
  • Providing certain information (for example your name, ID number and contact details) is generally necessary for Zanka to enter into or perform a management agreement, lease, or provide a Service you have requested. If you do not provide it, we may be unable to provide that Service;
  • Other information, such as optional details on a contact form, is provided voluntarily; and
  • We collect and process personal information under the authority of, among others, the common law of contract, applicable rental housing and consumer protection legislation, tax and financial record-keeping legislation, and POPIA itself.

4. The Eight Conditions for Lawful Processing

POPIA requires that personal information be processed lawfully and in a manner that does not infringe your privacy. Zanka applies POPIA's eight conditions for lawful processing:

  1. Accountability — we are responsible for complying with these conditions.
  2. Processing limitation — we process personal information lawfully, minimally, and on the basis of your consent or another basis permitted by POPIA.
  3. Purpose specification — we collect personal information for specific, defined purposes, as set out in our Privacy Policy.
  4. Further processing limitation — we do not process personal information for a purpose incompatible with the original purpose, without further justification.
  5. Information quality — we take reasonable steps to keep personal information accurate, complete and up to date.
  6. Openness — we maintain documentation of our processing activities and provide notices such as this one.
  7. Security safeguards — we implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (see section 10 of our Privacy Policy).
  8. Data subject participation — you may request access to, and correction or deletion of, your personal information (see section 6 below).

5. Recipients and Cross-Border Transfers

We may share personal information with the operators and third parties listed in section 7 of our Privacy Policy, including service providers that may process data outside South Africa, such as our cloud hosting, email and messaging providers. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of protection, in line with section 72 of POPIA.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal information Zanka holds about you;
  • request correction or deletion of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
  • object, on reasonable grounds, to the processing of your personal information;
  • object to your personal information being used for direct marketing, and to withdraw consent to processing, at any time;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing which affects you significantly; and
  • submit a complaint to the Information Regulator regarding an alleged infringement of your rights under POPIA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details in section 2. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

7. Lodging a Complaint with the Information Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how Zanka has handled your personal information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa):

Address: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191
General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za
POPIA complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
Telephone: 010 023 5200 (Toll-free: 0800 017 160)
Website: inforegulator.org.za

8. Updates to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in our processing activities or in POPIA itself. The current version will always be available at zankagroup.co.za/popia.html.
