Refund & Cancellation Policy

Zanka Group (Pty) Ltd

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Quick reference

For customers making a payment through the Zanka website:

Before service begins
You may request cancellation and a refund, subject to any disclosed non-refundable charges and costs already incurred.
After service begins
You may cancel, but Zanka may retain or charge amounts relating to work already performed and costs already incurred.
After service is completed
Payments for completed services are generally non-refundable, subject to applicable law and the terms of the relevant agreement.
Recurring property-management services
Cancellation is governed by the applicable management agreement and its notice provisions.
Maintenance services
Cancellation may be subject to contractor call-out charges, materials purchased and work already completed.
Refund requests
Email admin@zankagroup.co.za with your payment/order details and reason for the request.

1. General Principle

Zanka aims to provide customers with clear and fair cancellation and refund arrangements. Refund eligibility depends on:

  • The nature of the service;
  • Whether the service has already commenced;
  • Whether the service has already been completed;
  • Any third-party costs already incurred;
  • The applicable service agreement or quotation; and
  • Any rights available to the customer under applicable South African law.

Where a specific written agreement contains a different cancellation or refund provision, that provision will apply to the relevant service.

2. Cancellation Before a Service Commences

Where a customer cancels a service before Zanka has commenced providing the service, the customer may be eligible for a full or partial refund, subject to:

  • The applicable service agreement;
  • Any non-refundable fees disclosed before payment;
  • Third-party costs already incurred; and
  • Any work already performed by Zanka.

Where no work or third-party expenditure has occurred, Zanka will generally endeavour to refund the amount paid.

3. Cancellation After a Service Has Commenced

Where a customer cancels after Zanka has commenced providing the service, the customer may be charged for:

  • Work already completed;
  • Time already spent;
  • Costs already incurred;
  • Third-party services already commissioned;
  • Materials already purchased; and
  • Other reasonable costs directly attributable to the customer's instruction.

Any remaining eligible balance may be refunded.

4. Completed Services

Once a service has been fully performed, the customer will generally not be entitled to a refund simply because they subsequently decide that they no longer require the service. This does not limit any rights the customer may have where the service was not provided as agreed or where applicable law provides otherwise.

5. Property Management and Recurring Services

Where Zanka provides property management, administration or other recurring services, cancellation will be governed primarily by the applicable management agreement or mandate. Any notice period, termination fee or other cancellation requirement will be disclosed in that agreement. Payments relating to services already provided before termination are generally not refundable.

6. Tenant Placement and Leasing Services

Where Zanka provides tenant placement or leasing services, fees may relate to work performed during the tenant acquisition and placement process. Once the agreed placement service has been substantially performed, the applicable placement fee may become payable and may not be refundable, subject to the relevant agreement and applicable law. Any special placement guarantee or replacement-tenant arrangement will only apply where expressly included in the relevant agreement.

7. Maintenance and Contractor Services

Where Zanka arranges maintenance or other property-related work:

  • Cancellation before work commences may be refundable, subject to costs already incurred;
  • Cancellation after a contractor has been instructed may result in charges for cancellation or call-out costs;
  • Materials already purchased may be charged to the customer;
  • Work already completed will generally be payable; and
  • Any applicable third-party or contractor cancellation charges will be passed on to the customer.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email: admin@zankagroup.co.za

The request should include:

  • Customer name;
  • Contact details;
  • Invoice or order number;
  • Payment reference;
  • Date of payment;
  • Amount paid;
  • Reason for the refund request; and
  • Any supporting documentation.

Zanka may request additional information reasonably required to assess the request.

9. Refund Processing

Once a refund request has been approved, Zanka will process the refund using the appropriate payment method. For payments processed through PayFast, the available refund mechanism and applicable processing requirements may depend on the payment method used. PayFast currently provides merchants with functionality to process full or partial refunds, and refund processing may take additional time to reflect in the customer's account depending on the payment method and banking institution. Zanka will provide reasonable confirmation once a refund has been processed.

10. Refund Limitations

A refund will not exceed the amount originally paid for the relevant transaction. Zanka will not provide duplicate refunds for the same transaction. Where a customer has already received a refund through another method, Zanka may reject or adjust a subsequent refund request relating to the same payment. Where a payment provider's refund process requires the refund to be returned through the original payment method, Zanka will follow that process where applicable.

11. Chargebacks

Customers should contact Zanka first if they believe a payment was made incorrectly, duplicated or should be refunded. Where a refund has already been issued, the customer should not initiate a duplicate chargeback for the same amount. Zanka reserves the right to provide transaction records, invoices, service records, communications and other relevant evidence to the applicable payment provider or financial institution where a chargeback is initiated.
