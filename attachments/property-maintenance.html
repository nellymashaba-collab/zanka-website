Property Maintenance Management in Johannesburg

Zanka coordinates trusted contractors, competitive quotes and efficient project management for everything from routine repairs to larger maintenance projects on your Johannesburg rental property.

What We Coordinate

Deferred maintenance is one of the fastest ways a rental property loses value, and one of the most common landlord frustrations is chasing contractors for quotes that never arrive. Zanka manages that process end to end: getting competitive quotes, coordinating the work, and keeping the owner informed.

How Pricing Works

Basic maintenance coordination is included in every Zanka property management package. For larger maintenance projects (a renovation, a full repaint, a significant repair), Zanka charges a project management fee of 10% of the project value, on top of the contractor's own quote. You approve the quote before any work begins. Full pricing is on our Pricing page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Zanka manage maintenance for rental properties?

Yes. Zanka coordinates plumbing, electrical, painting, cleaning, general repairs and emergency maintenance through a network of trusted contractors, from getting quotes through to project completion.

How much does property maintenance management cost?

Basic maintenance coordination is included in Zanka's property management packages. Larger maintenance projects are managed under a separate project management fee of 10% of the project value. See our Pricing page for full details.

Who approves maintenance work on my property?

Zanka obtains competitive quotes and coordinates the work, keeping the owner informed and seeking approval for spend above agreed thresholds, in line with the applicable management agreement.

Does Zanka handle emergency repairs?

Yes, emergency maintenance is part of Zanka's maintenance coordination service, alongside routine plumbing, electrical, painting, cleaning and general repairs.

Have maintenance that needs sorting out?

Tell us what's needed and we'll coordinate quotes and get it done.
